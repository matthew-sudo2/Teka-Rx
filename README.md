# TekaRx 💊

> **Teka muna. Let's check that prescription first.**

TekaRx is an Explainable AI Clinical Decision Support System designed to identify potential medication safety risks before they become adverse drug events. The system assists healthcare professionals by analyzing medication regimens, patient characteristics, and evidence from trusted pharmacovigilance sources to generate interpretable risk assessments.

> **This project is intended for research and educational purposes. TekaRx does not replace licensed healthcare professionals and must not be used as an autonomous medical decision-making system.**

---

## Vision

Medication errors and adverse drug events remain one of the leading preventable causes of patient harm worldwide. Many dangerous interactions are not immediately apparent during routine clinical workflows.

**TekaRx aims to answer one simple question:**

> **"Teka muna... ligtas ba ang kombinasyon ng mga gamot na ito?"**

Rather than replacing physicians or pharmacists, TekaRx provides explainable risk assessments that help support safer clinical decisions.

---

## Objectives

* Build a reproducible medication safety data pipeline.
* Detect high-risk medication regimens using machine learning.
* Provide interpretable predictions using Explainable AI.
* Integrate trusted pharmacovigilance and drug-reference data.
* Develop a clinician-friendly dashboard for risk visualization.

---

## Planned Features

* Medication normalization and standardization
* Drug interaction knowledge graph
* Polypharmacy risk prediction
* Adverse drug event risk estimation
* SHAP-based feature explanations
* Evidence retrieval from trusted medical sources
* Interactive clinical dashboard
* Experiment tracking and reproducible ML pipelines

Clinical decisions should always be made by qualified healthcare professionals.
